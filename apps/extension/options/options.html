How to Connect Your Account

To get your access token:

  1. Open the web app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Click "Copy Access Token".
  4. Click the extension icon and paste the token in the login view.

Tokens expire after 15 minutes. If the extension shows "Not signed in", get a fresh token from Settings.
